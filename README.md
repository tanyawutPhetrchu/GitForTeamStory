## Our Wonderful Story

1. Once upon a time, there was a **castle**.  
In a fantasy world or a sci-fi world? Let's mix them together.
This is the kingdom of "Tesla," ruled by a king named Elon Mxxx (take a guess).

It is a highly advanced kingdom where people live in a medieval fantasy style, blending a touch of magic with technology. 
The townspeople lived in peace until one day, a dragon-like creature appeared in the forest near the kingdom, causing panic among the villagers.

King Exxx xxx sent a team of knights named "Team A+", consisting of Abdul, Alex, Abraham, and Adolf the Healer, to deal with it.
When Team A+ arrived at where the creature was, they found that...  
   ![castle](img/castle.png)


2. Then suddenly, a **dragon** appeared… but maybe it was actually a **robot**?  
   (Nobody is sure yet.)

   ![A green dragon walking toward the mountain](img/dragon.png)

   It was green, and it could breathe fire. At that moment it was walking straight toward the mountain, making a loud *creak, clank, thud* as it went, so we couldn’t help wondering whether it was a robot. But that didn’t stop us from trying to talk to it.

   "Hey, Dragon! Where are you headed?” we asked.

   It didn’t seem to hear us, because it was so tall. So it slowly bent down to look at us, and said, "Huh?"

   We shouted again, but this time it said, "Speak softly! My ears are about to burst!"

   So we whispered, and at last we got an answer: "I need to poop. I’m looking for a place to go."

   Before long, the dragon dropped its load, and the smell spread across the whole forest. It smelled like papaya salad with salted crab and fermented fish sauce. That made us realize this dragon might be a robot that eats som tam, which was entirely possible.

   But suddenly, we heard someone shouting from far away, loud, but too distant for us to make out a single word.

3. The scientist shouted something very important but nobody wrote it down.

   ![scientist shouting](img/scientist.png)

   A scientist who had been doing research in this forest, but who was standing on a different cliff top, shouted out an important word. A few of us managed to piece together that it was about something significant, or perhaps even **treasure**. While he was shouting, everyone got ready and pulled out their iPads to write that word down, but nobody was fast enough to catch it.

   But it seemed that one of us had managed to write it down in time. So I walked over to her and asked, "Did you get it? Can I take a look?"

   She nodded and handed her iPad to me. What I saw on the screen right then was a picture of that scientist shouting. I looked back and forth between her and the iPad in my hands, and I understood right away that she had drawn a picture. All of us could only burst out laughing.

   And at this moment, the sky was beginning to change color, a sign that we should hurry and set off on our journey again.

4. After that, everyone got lost (or maybe teleported?) and somehow there was a **treasure chest**…  
   ![treasure chest](img/treasure_large.png)

   As the sky turned purple, Team A+ continued their journey through the forest. Suddenly, a strange blue light appeared beneath their feet. Before anyone could react, the ground shook, the trees began to spin, and—WHOOSH!—everyone disappeared.

When they opened their eyes, they found themselves in a mysterious underground cave filled with glowing crystals and floating rocks. Abdul looked around and said, “Wait a minute… Weren’t we just in the forest?”

Alex checked his iPad, but the screen displayed only one message: LOCATION UNKNOWN.

“Great! Even our technology has no idea where we are,” Abraham complained.

Meanwhile, Adolf the Healer noticed something shining behind a giant rock. He walked closer and discovered an ancient treasure chest covered in strange symbols.

“Guys! Hurry!!! Come and look at this!” he shouted.

The team gathered around the chest. It was locked, and a small dragon-shaped keyhole glowed green. As soon as Abdul touched the lid, a mysterious voice echoed through the cave.

“Only those who know the way can claim what lies within.”

Everyone looked at one another in confusion.

"which way? We don't even know where we are!” Alex exclaimed.

Suddenly, the treasure chest opened by itself. Inside, they found a golden compass, several ancient coins, and a rolled-up piece of paper.

Abraham picked up the paper and carefully unfolded it. It appeared to be a map, but something was terribly wrong…

5. Someone found a map, but it had no directions.  
   ![The Map](img/map.png)

   Abraham spread the map across a flat rock, and everyone gathered around to examine it. It showed the kingdom of Tesla, the mysterious forest, a giant mountain, and even the underground cave where they were standing. However, there were no arrows, no roads, and absolutely no directions.

“This map is useless!” Abdul exclaimed. “How are we supposed to find the treasure?”

“Maybe we need to use magic,” Adolf the Healer suggested.

Alex held up his iPad and scanned the map. To everyone's surprise, the screen began glowing green, and a holographic dragon appeared above it.

The dragon spoke in a deep, mechanical voice: “The map does not show where you should go. It shows what you must discover.”

Suddenly, three symbols appeared on the map: a flame, a gear, and an eye. Beneath them, a message slowly materialized:

“Find the fire that never burns, the machine that never moves, and the eye that sees without looking. Only then will the path to the ultimate treasure reveal itself.”

The team fell silent.

“Fire that never burns? A machine that never moves? An eye that sees without looking?” Alex repeated. “What does any of that even mean?”

Before anyone could answer, the golden compass began spinning wildly. Its needle stopped pointing north and instead pointed directly toward the mountain where the green dragon had been heading.

From somewhere deep inside the cave, a familiar sound echoed through the darkness:

“Creak… clank… THUD!”

Abdul slowly turned toward the cave entrance. And suddenly found something that never want to see.

“Guys… I think our dragon is back.”

And just like that, Team A+ realized that their adventure was far from over.
